An entry can trigger several findings. Warnings alone do not quarantine a record.

The question

When a data feed contains a bad identifier or an invalid reference, which entries can still be used? The report separates records with direct errors from records that depend on them.

Trace a synthetic failure

Community clinic batch feed-2-01. The numbers below are zero-based entry indexes in the audit ledger.

  1. Entry 0 · Patient · Direct quarantine. The birth date 1982-02-31 fails the BIRTH_DATE rule.
  2. Entry 1 · Encounter · Dependency quarantine. Its subject points to entry 0.
  3. Entry 2 · Observation · Dependency quarantine. The weight observation points to encounter entry 1 and patient entry 0.

A reviewer would start with the patient record before revalidating the linked entries.

Methods and implementation
  1. Synthetic FHIR R4 bundles
  2. Structure and rule checks
  3. Dependency quarantine
  4. SQLite lineage
  5. Quality report

The pipeline checks selected Patient, Encounter, and Observation fields and references. It stores batch and entry identifiers, records findings, and exports aggregates. The local policy includes a limited laboratory terminology set and measurement checks.

Limitations

All feeds are generated fixtures. Rules cover a deliberately bounded subset of R4 and local policy. Real feeds may require profile validation, a terminology service, external-reference resolution, and privacy and governance review.

Follow-up validation

A feed owner could use the counts to trace a cluster of failed entries to its upstream cause. For wider deployment, run the rules alongside a full profile validator and test error handling against more representative integrations.
