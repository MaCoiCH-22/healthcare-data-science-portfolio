The problem

Hospital workbooks vary in layout and data quality. Before reviewing a log, an analyst has to find the relevant sheets, identify the fields, and resolve irregularities in the file.

What I built

The application inspects incoming workbooks, supports analyst-guided field mapping, and automates the repeated cleanup and standardization steps. It flags exceptions for review and produces a consistent log for the analyst to check.

How a workbook moves through the application

  1. Inspect the workbook. Identify its sheets and structure before applying a mapping.
  2. Guide the mapping. The analyst confirms how source fields correspond to the standardized log.
  3. Scrub and validate. The application applies cleanup rules, standardizes the log, and surfaces exceptions.
  4. Review the output. The analyst resolves exceptions and decides when the log is ready.

Why the analyst stays in control

Similar column names can mean different things across hospital files. Analyst review keeps those decisions explicit, especially when the workbook structure or data is ambiguous.

Scope

Employer source code, client workbooks, and patient data are confidential.
