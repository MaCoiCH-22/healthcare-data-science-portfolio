About MaCoi

My path into
healthcare analytics

I’m a Reimbursement Analyst at Genesa Reimbursement Group, where I built a Python application that automates Medicare bad debt log scrubbing. It inspects incoming Excel workbooks, handles inconsistent layouts and messy data, and turns them into standardized logs for review. Analysts guide how fields are mapped, review exceptions, and decide when the output is ready.

In clinical research at DelRicht, I checked trial data against source records and worked with clinical teams to resolve discrepancies. I’ve also supported workforce compliance reporting, managed donor data for the Doll & Toy Fund, and worked in pharmacy operations and laboratory testing.

I earned a B.S. in Chemistry from Dillard University and pursued pharmacy coursework at Xavier University of Louisiana. I’m now completing an M.S. in Applied Data Science at the University of San Diego, expected summer 2027. I’m interested in healthcare and clinical analytics roles where I can build on my experience with data quality and apply my graduate training in statistics and modeling.
