About MaCoi

A scientific foundation.
A healthcare focus.

I earned my B.S. in Chemistry from Dillard University in 2022 and pursued pharmacy coursework at Xavier University of Louisiana. Lab work, pharmacy operations, and clinical research data coordination gave me experience with how information moves between scientific and healthcare teams.

I work as a Reimbursement Analyst at Genesa Reimbursement Group. I validate hospital data in SQL Server, reconcile Medicare reporting totals, and build automated checks for Excel workbooks in Python. I’m pursuing an M.S. in Applied Data Science at the University of San Diego and expect to complete it in summer 2027.

My current interests include clinical research analytics, pharmaceutical data science, and reliable healthcare data systems.
